Community Scale & Data Ownership

Why Self-Hosted Autonomy Beats Cloud Silos

Across decades of scaling developer platforms, community infrastructure, and technical forums at Unity and Perforce, I have learned a core truth. Platforms live and die by user trust. When companies lock developer data and community contributions behind opaque, proprietary silos, engagement rots.

The Operating Principles I Follow

  • Data Ownership First: Communities and developers must retain control over their contributions, code, and telemetry. Renting space on someone else's closed cloud without data portability is a ticking time bomb.
  • Listening to the Edge: As Larry Hryb noted, the key is having an instinct for what the community actually needs instead of assuming what they want. That requires clean feedback loops, not corporate echo chambers.
  • Resilient Architecture: Building systems that are modular, transparent, and capable of operating with local-first reliability.

"If you do not respect your community's data ownership and autonomy, you are not building a platform. You are just operating a toll booth."
