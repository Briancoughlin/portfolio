Primary Engagement Metric Framework

Designing Rigorous Telemetry for Community Health

Community platforms are designed to be more than static repositories; they are environments where creators share, learn, and build knowledge together. Measuring engagement effectively requires recognizing that both content creation and consumption are vital to overall ecosystem health.

A robust telemetry framework must capture three core behaviors:

  • Creation: Starting valuable discussions, posting replies, and providing verified solutions.
  • Consumption: Reading, reacting to, and bookmarking content, even without posting.
  • Depth of Participation: Interactions that are relevant and impactful, moving well beyond surface-level clicks.

The North Star Metric: Engaged User Rate (EUR)

Definition: The percentage of Monthly Active Users (MAU) who either create content or meaningfully interact with existing content in a given monthly period.

“Meaningfully interact” is defined by strict quality thresholds:

  • Reading ≥ 3 distinct topics with an average dwell time ≥ 15 seconds.
  • Posting at least once (topic or reply).
  • Contributing a positive reaction, bookmark, or marking a verified solution.

Why EUR Works

  • Balanced: Assigns equal structural weight to creation and consumption.
  • Retention Signal: Consumption reliably precedes creation; both predict long-term user stickiness.
  • Quality Filter: Incorporates dwell time and action thresholds to prevent metric inflation from shallow page views or bot noise.

Implementation Architecture

Data Warehouse Sources (e.g., BigQuery Mart):

  • topic_views_raw: Tracking topic view counts and session dwell time.
  • posts_raw: Monitoring new threads and replies.
  • engagement_fact: Aggregating likes, bookmarks, and accepted solutions.
  • user_activity_fact: Calculating baseline MAU counts.

Formula Logic (Monthly Grain)

consumed_flag = viewed ≥ 3 topics AND avg dwell ≥ 15 sec
created_flag  = ≥ 1 post/topic
interacted_flag = ≥ 1 reaction (like/bookmark/solution)
engaged_flag = consumed_flag OR created_flag OR interacted_flag
EUR% = (engaged_users / total_mau) × 100

Supporting Diagnostics

While EUR serves as the primary health metric, these supporting diagnostics provide operational clarity:

  1. Creation-to-Consumption Ratio: Tracks the balance of engaged users who create versus those who only consume, preventing lurker-heavy skews.
  2. Depth of Session Engagement: Measures average engaged actions per user per month (posts, likes, replies, solutions given) to evaluate intensity.
  3. Return Rate of Engaged Users: Measures month-over-month retention of active cohorts.
  4. Engagement by Persona: Granular tracking across cohorts (new users, students, professionals, MVPs, staff).

Trade-Off Analysis

Advantages Watchouts & Mitigations
Captures the full spectrum of user activity (reading + writing). Requires rigorous thresholding to filter out low-value default home page clicks.
Directly aligns with platform activation and retention goals. Requires clean event instrumentation in data warehouses during initial setup.
Applicable across personalization, gamification, and safety initiatives. Does not capture qualitative sentiment on its own; requires complementary survey telemetry.

Implementation Roadmap

  1. Model Deployment: Establish EUR and supporting measures within data warehouses.
  2. Dashboard Integration: Visualize EUR overall, by persona, and by category with trend lines and drill-downs.
  3. Executive Reporting: Incorporate EUR into monthly platform KPIs for leadership review.
  4. Threshold Iteration: Continuously calibrate dwell time and view counts against real user patterns.
